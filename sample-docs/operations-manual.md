# Northstar Works — Operations manual

Fictional sample document for Groundwork. Effective 1 October 2026.

## Security incidents

Report suspected security incidents immediately to the IT service desk, including lost laptops, suspicious emails, or unexpected account activity. Do not delete suspicious messages or logs. Disconnect a potentially compromised device from the network and wait for IT instructions. Never share passwords or verification codes with anyone, including people claiming to be IT staff.

## Account security

Use unique passwords of at least 12 characters and enable multi-factor authentication on all company services. Store passwords in the approved password manager. Lock your screen whenever you leave your workstation. IT reviews access each quarter.

## Visitors

All visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge. The host must escort visitors in non-public areas. Arrange visits at least one working day in advance. Return badges to reception when leaving.

## Meeting rooms

Book meeting rooms through the internal calendar. Release a booking if the meeting is cancelled. Meetings should end five minutes before the reserved time ends. Clear whiteboards and remove confidential papers before leaving.

## Equipment support

Submit equipment problems to the IT service desk with the asset number and a description of the issue. Standard support hours are 09:00 to 18:00, Monday through Friday. Urgent incidents outside those hours go to the on-call IT contact listed in the employee portal.

## Onboarding

Managers submit new-starter requests to IT at least five working days before the start date. IT prepares a laptop, account, and access badge. New starters complete security awareness training within their first week. People Operations schedules a check-in after 30 days.
