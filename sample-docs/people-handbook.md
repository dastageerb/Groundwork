# Northstar Works — People handbook

This handbook is entirely fictional, written for evaluating Groundwork. Effective 1 October 2026. All amounts, policies, and names are examples.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year, prorated in their first year. Employees can request annual leave after completing their first 90 days. Submit requests to your manager at least two weeks before the first day of leave. The manager approves requests within five working days. Up to five unused days can be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days each calendar year. Notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive working days of sickness. Unused sick days do not carry over.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the team's agreed time zone. Working from a different country requires written approval from People Operations before travel. Use the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.

## Equipment allowance

New employees may claim a one-time home office allowance of USD 300 after passing probation. Submit itemized receipts through the expense process. Chairs, monitors, keyboards, and ergonomic accessories qualify. Personal entertainment devices do not qualify.

## Learning and development

Each employee has a USD 500 annual learning budget. Obtain manager approval before buying a course or booking a conference. The budget resets on 1 January and does not roll over. Job-related courses, books, and professional memberships are eligible.

## Working hours

The standard work week is 40 hours, Monday through Friday. Agree flexible start and finish times with your manager while respecting core collaboration hours. Overtime must be approved in writing before it is worked.
