# Northstar Works — Finance guide

Fictional sample document for Groundwork. Effective 1 October 2026. All policies in this document are visible to all employees.

## Expense claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase. Include an itemized receipt, business purpose, cost center, and manager approval. Finance processes approved claims in the next payroll cycle. Claims without receipts require written approval from the Finance lead.

## Business travel

Get written manager approval before booking travel. Flights must be economy class for trips shorter than six hours. Use the approved booking portal. The daily meal allowance is USD 50; alcohol is not reimbursable. The hotel limit is USD 180 per night excluding taxes. Exceptions require Finance lead approval before booking.

## Purchasing

Purchases under USD 250 require manager approval. Purchases from USD 250 through USD 2,000 require department head approval. Purchases above USD 2,000 also require Finance lead approval. Do not split a purchase into multiple claims to bypass approval limits.

| Purchase value | Required approval |
| --- | --- |
| Under USD 250 | Manager |
| USD 250 to USD 2,000 | Department head |
| Above USD 2,000 | Department head and Finance lead |

## Payroll

Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. Submit bank account changes to Finance at least seven working days before payday. Payslips are available in the employee portal on payday. Report a payroll discrepancy within five working days.
